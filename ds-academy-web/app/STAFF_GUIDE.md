# Admin Panel — staff guide

DS Science Academy. Written for the office; no technical background needed.

---

## Signing in

Go to **dsscienceacademy.com/admin** and enter your email and password.

You stay signed in for 12 hours, then it asks again. Use **Sign out** at the top
right when you leave a shared computer.

Forgot the password? Ask whoever holds the Super Admin account to set a new one
in **Staff & Roles**.

---

## The layout

- **Left sidebar** — every section you are allowed to open.
- **Top bar** — the page name, a link to view the live website, and Sign out.
- **Save** — nothing is live until you press Save. A small dark message appears
  at the bottom of the screen to confirm.

Anything marked **PRO** in purple is part of the Pro plan.

## Everyday jobs

### Add a topper

**Toppers / Results › + Topper**

Name, photo, exam, rank or score, year. Tick **Show on home page** for the ones
you want in the home strip — four to eight looks best. Save.

A photo helps a lot. Portrait, face centred, any size; it gets resized for you.

### Post a notice

**News & Events › + Notice**

Title, type (Notice or Event), the text, and set Status to **Published**. Leave
it on Draft while you are still writing — drafts are invisible to visitors.

**Pin to top** keeps it first in the yellow strip across the home page. Pin one
thing at a time, or it stops meaning anything.

### Upload the brochure or a paper

**Downloads › + Download**

Title, category (Brochure, Syllabus, PYQ, Other), then upload the PDF. The list
shows how many times each file has been downloaded — useful for knowing which
brochure is actually working.

### Change the home banner

**Hero Banners › + Banner**

Headline, sub-line, a wide image (at least 1600 px across), and where the button
should go — `/admissions`, `/results`, and so on. Set Status to **Live**.

Order 1 shows first. Slides rotate every six seconds.

### Handle enquiries

**Admissions / Enquiries**

Every website form lands here, newest first. Click **Edit** to record what
happened and change the status.

**Export CSV** downloads the whole list for Excel.

### Update contact details or colours

**Settings** — institute name, address, phone numbers, WhatsApp number, social
links, logo, brand colours and the default SEO text. Save at the bottom.

The four buttons at the end of that page — Stats strip, Why choose us,
Testimonials, Facilities — edit the blocks on the home page.

## Pro sections

### Lead CRM

A board with four columns: New → Contacted → Demo Booked → Admitted.

- **Drag a card** to move a lead along.
- **Click a card** to see everything, assign it to a counsellor, set a follow-up
  date, and add notes.
- A card with a red edge is a follow-up you have missed.

### Course Packages

**Course Packages** — tabs across the top for Class 11, Class 12, Dropper and
Foundation. Each card is one package: type (Recorded, Live, Test Series), what
is included, original price, discounted price and the discount percentage.

These appear on the course page on the website.

### Rank Predictor

**Rank Predictor** — the cut-off data behind the public predictor.

Do not type rows one by one. Open **Bulk import**, paste the whole spreadsheet
as CSV with a header row, and import. Tick *Replace* when you are loading a
corrected version of a year you already uploaded.

Predictions always use the most recent year on file for each exam.

### Broadcast

**Broadcast** — one WhatsApp or SMS message to a group of leads.

Pick the segment (the number in brackets is how many people will get it), the
channel, and write the message. You are asked to confirm before anything sends.

If the panel says **Dry-run mode**, no gateway is connected yet: the message is
recorded and you can see who would have received it, but nothing goes out.

### Staff & Roles

**Staff & Roles › + Staff account**

- **Super Admin** — everything.
- **Counsellor** — leads, enquiries, demos, broadcast.
- **Editor** — website content only.

Untick **Can sign in** to suspend someone; they are signed out immediately.
Leave the password blank when editing to keep the current one.

The last Super Admin cannot be deleted or demoted, so nobody can lock everyone
out by accident.

## Things worth knowing

- **Nothing is hardcoded.** Every word, number and photo on the public site is
  editable from here.
- **Draft vs Published** applies to News and Blog. Drafts are private.
- **Visible on website** ticks apply to Toppers, Faculty, Courses, Gallery,
  Facilities and FAQs. Unticking hides something without deleting it — safer
  than deleting when you are unsure.
- **Deleting is permanent.** You are asked to confirm; there is no undo.
- **Images** — landscape for banners and facilities, portrait for people. Upload
  the original; it is optimised automatically. Limit is 8 MB.
- **The panel works on a phone.** The sidebar hides behind the ☰ button.

## If something looks wrong

1. Refresh the page.
2. Check you pressed Save and saw the confirmation.
3. Check the item is Published or ticked Visible.
4. The public site caches for a few minutes — wait, then refresh.

Still wrong? Note what you clicked and what happened, and send it to whoever
maintains the site.
